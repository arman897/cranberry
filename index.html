Our Story

A taste of
perfection in
every dish

Aarzoo Cranberry Restaurant brings four kitchens under one roof: Indian curries and tandoor, wok-fired Chinese, wood-fired pizza and pasta, and a bar of mocktails and cold drinks — all built around the same cranberry signature that gives the place its name.

We're located at NS Tower on Pachamba Main Road, Bisanpur, Giridih — a multicuisine table for family dinners, quick lunches, and everything in between.
